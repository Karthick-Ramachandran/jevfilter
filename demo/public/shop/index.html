What just happened

  1. Your sentence went to the server. Code read the prices and ratings, and Jev picked each filter from this store's own list of categories, colors, and brands.
  2. JevFilter checked the result against the store's schema. Nothing outside that list can get through.
  3. The store's normal product query ran with those filters, the same one the sidebar uses. No SQL was written by a model.

Changing the sidebar yourself doesn't call the model at all. For every step in detail, open the helpdesk demo with the X-ray panel.

Trailhead and its products are made up. What you type, along with the store's list of filters, is sent to TypeSafe's Jev model to interpret.
